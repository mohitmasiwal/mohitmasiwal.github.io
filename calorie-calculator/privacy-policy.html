Privacy Policy

Calorie Calculator: TDEE, BMR (package com.caloriecalculator.tdee)
Developer: MOHIT AGENCIES · Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Calorie Calculator app ("the app") uses, why, and your choices. In short: the app has no account system and no server of its own. Your details and logs are stored only on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device only

  • Your profile: the name you type, sex, age, height, weight, type of daily work, usual steps, usual workouts, goal (lose, maintain or gain), pace, goal weight and units. Used only to calculate your calories.
  • Your logs: steps, workouts and food calories you enter for each day, and the weights you record.

We (MOHIT AGENCIES) do not collect, receive, sell or share this information. It never leaves your device. The app does not read data from other apps, sensors, location or health services: it only uses what you type in.

2. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to load ads. The app asks for no other permissions.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can show your approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions. Your profile and logs are never shared with Google or any ad network. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice anytime from the app menu (⋮ → Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Learn how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete workouts and weights inside the app, or delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Apps → Calorie Calculator → Storage → Clear data. Because we do not hold any copy, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

5. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Health information

Calories, BMR, BMI and macros are estimates for healthy adults and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet if you are pregnant, under 18 or have a health condition.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
