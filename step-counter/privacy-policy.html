Privacy Policy

Step Counter: Pedometer & Run (package com.stepcounter.runtracker)
Developer: MOHIT AGENCIES · Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Step Counter app ("the app") uses, why, and your choices. In short: the app has no account system and no server of its own. Your steps, runs and settings are stored only on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device only

  • Your name (the first name you type when you start), used only to greet you in the app.
  • Step data: steps per day and per hour, your daily goal and streaks, counted with your phone's motion/step sensors.
  • Run data: start time, duration, distance, per-km splits and the GPS route of runs you choose to track.

We (MOHIT AGENCIES) do not collect, receive, sell or share this information. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share an image (for example a run card) using your phone's share menu.

2. Permissions

  • Physical activity: to count your steps with the phone's step sensor.
  • Location (precise): used only while you are tracking a run, to measure distance and draw your route. The app does not use location in the background when no run is active.
  • Notifications: to show today's steps, the running timer, and a per-kilometre update.
  • Internet: to load map tiles and ads.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice anytime from the app menu (⋮ → Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Learn how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Maps

Run maps use map tiles from OpenStreetMap. When a map is shown, your device requests the tiles for the area on screen from the tile server, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a run inside the app, or delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Apps → Step Counter → Storage → Clear data. Because we do not hold any copy, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Health information

Steps, distance and calories are estimates for general fitness only and are not medical advice.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
